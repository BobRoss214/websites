Traditional fall school tours

School field trips to an organic farm

Our school tours give preschool and elementary school students a fun, hands-on introduction to organic farming. Students learn how food is grown while exploring the farm.

Sign up for a tour Email Vanessa

What students do

Your tour includes

  • Educational farm talk tailored for students
  • A small pumpkin to take home
  • Corn pit
  • Wagon ride through the woods
  • Meet and feed the farm animals
  • Unlimited access to playground areas
  • Educational scavenger hunt
  • Picnic tables in the shaded Party Patch for lunch or snacks

Teacher questions

Booking a school tour

How do I book a school tour?

Fill out the sign-up form and we’ll contact you shortly. You can also email Vanessa at vanessa@wiseacresorganic.com with questions.

How much does a school tour cost?

School tours are $11 per student. Accompanying parents and siblings ages 3 and older pay the $11 admission fee if they attend.

What is the minimum group size?

The minimum group size for a traditional fall school tour is 100 students.

What grades are school tours for?

Our school tours are designed for preschool and elementary school students.

Where is the farm?

Wise Acres Organic Farm is at 4701 Hartis Road in Indian Trail, North Carolina, just outside Charlotte.
