# Notes from helper desk-live: the desk in a pretend rush (10 Nov 2026)

Branch `desklive` in /tmp/desklive-clone (from integ a21f60f; it contains the kitchen-live engine commit e09af6c, merged). Nothing here is real: pretend families, sample words, nothing sent.

## What the builder asked, and what is on the screen
"Pretend data flowing so Miss Cathy can see how it all works" on Mrs. Cathy and Rob's desk, calm enough to read, the existing answers kept, and the tour explaining it.

- `farm-desk.html` has a box **Right now** at the top (`data-tour="desk-live"`): the rush's own strip (`DemoLive.strip()`, the same control as the kitchen: Pretend rush, On / Off, Calm / Busy / Rush), four counters (**Waiting for you**, **Photos and reviews to look at**, **Answered today**, **People waiting for news**; the old three tiles moved into this box, "Answered today" is new and is not a link) and a feed **Just now** (the last six things that happened, newest first, with "just now" / "3 min ago").
- The pretend families are in the store's normal lists (`live: true`, `fam`). The desk reads them with `S.list(coll, { live: true })` and prints `fam` where it used to print the signed-in family (cards, feed, "This week"). The family's own screens never see them.
- Calm: the cards stay in the order they came in (new ones last, oldest first), the card being read never moves, a note being typed keeps its words, focus and cursor (new cards are added around it, the New mark is taken off in place after 30 s), the page keeps what is on the screen where it is when cards above change (`holdPlace`), an answered card leaves **Waiting for you** at once (its result is the first card under **Answered**, the latest 6; the focus goes to the next card's heading, or to "Nothing is waiting"), arrivals are said in a polite region of their own in batches (one message every 4 s), a small button "Show N new cards" appears when new cards are out of sight, the tab title counts what waits while the rush is on, nothing flashes (one 2.4 s fade, once; none with reduced motion or `data-calm`). No sound on the desk.
- Counters and feed are read from the store (`reply.at`, `decided`, messages), never from memory: a reload or a second window says the same. "Answered today" is the bigger of that count and the engine's `DemoLive.tally('desk')` (each answer given on the desk adds one), so it never shrinks when the engine tidies old pretend records away. The words "just now / n min ago" count real time on this screen (an arrival that was already there when the screen opened is as old as the pretend clock says).
- Limits that keep it short: 24 waiting cards drawn (a line says how many more wait), 6 answered, 4 approvals done, "This week" 5 visits a day then "and N more visits", the feed keeps 40 lines (6 shown). The engine keeps only its newest 40 events of records.

## Decisions I took (the spec left them open)
1. Default OFF with the engine's big invitation (agreed with kitchen-live): the existing tests and the tour stay as they were, and the control is obvious on both screens.
2. Retiring an answered card at once (instead of keeping it in place with its result): with a rush the waiting list would fill with finished cards, and a card that disappears only when YOU answer, never by itself, cannot move under a finger. The old "answered card stays where it was" behaviour is gone; `ui.sent` is gone.
3. The feed is derived from the store (same on every tab and after a reload); the engine's `onEvent` is not used, so an arrival made by another tab shows too.
4. Tour: two cards (`ch10-desk-live` 1029, `ch10-desk-keep` 1030) written as what the desk DOES (the builder's rule of 10 Nov: no "new", "now", "changed"). The story cards switch the rush off and take the pretend families away first (`scene()` calls `rushOff()`; the live cards use `liveScene()` and `rushOn()`), so the story reads the same every time; the first live card starts the rush at Rush speed from nothing (four things already waiting). On the family's page there is no DemoLive: nothing is switched there, the next desk or kitchen card does it.
5. Orders of chapter 10 now go up in fours (1004 ... 1096) so cards can be added between others (commit 141880c, also in the kitchen branch as 0a9cb75); `tests/tour-demo-script.test.mjs` finds the original 24 cards by id (`OLD_IDS`).

## Not sure, and what I did
- The pretend clock of the rush (about 10:30 to 15:45, it follows the real one in the farm's hours) and the real clock differ when the rush is paused or started outside those hours: cards of pretend families say "Sent today at <pretend time>", answers are stamped with the real time. "Answered today" and the feed use real stamps for answers and the engine's own milliseconds for arrivals, so both are consistent on the screen; only the clock text in the box shows the pretend clock once the rush has made things.
- Pressing Yes twice quickly on one card: the second press finds the card gone (the store says no, the desk says "already answered").
- After the last photo/review is answered the focus goes to the "No photos or reviews to look at." line (shown whenever nothing waits, also under done cards).
- A suggestion to a pretend family is answered by the engine 5 to 18 rush seconds later (kitchen-live's rule); the desk says it politely and shows it under Answered.
