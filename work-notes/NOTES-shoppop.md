# Notes: the "What to buy" pop-up (helper shop-pop, 10 Nov 2026)

The builder (not the farm owner; the bosses are Mrs. Cathy and Rob) asked for two things in the Store: a card gets a little bigger, with a small animation, when it is pointed at, and a click (or tap) opens a box in the middle of the screen with the ingredients of that product. The farm has not given its real lists, so the lists are made up, and every box says in big letters "(This is not the real ingredients. This is just a preview.)".

## What it does (plain words)
- Pointing at a card with a list (or reaching it with the Tab key): the card grows about 7 percent (the two wide bars, ice cream and pizza, about 4.5 percent), lifts 4 px, its shadow gets deeper, its edge turns red and its picture tilts. Only `transform` moves, so nothing around it shifts. It sits above its neighbours. Pressed: a small squeeze. A mouse is needed for the grow (`@media (hover:hover)`), so a tap on a phone never leaves a card big. With "reduce motion" in the system, or after "Pause animations", nothing is scaled or tilted: only the shadow and the edge change.
- Click, tap, Enter or Space: ONE pop-up (a `div` with `role="dialog"`, not `<dialog>`) in the middle of the window, at most 34rem wide and 85 percent of the height (it scrolls inside if taller): a band in the colours of the card's group with the picture and the name, the price, the card's own note (copied from the card, so already translated), the big notice, "Ingredients" (or "Made of" for things that are not eaten or put on skin), the list as rounded chips, "Contains: ..." when the list has it, and a Close button. The X (44 px) and the Close button, Escape and a click outside shut it. It fades and scales in over 160 ms (not with reduced motion or "Pause animations").
- While it is open the page behind is out of reach of screen readers and of Tab (`aria-hidden` and `inert` on the other blocks of the page), does not scroll, and does not slide sideways when the scroll bar goes (the width of the bar is kept as padding on `<html>`). Tab and Shift+Tab stay inside. Focus goes to the X on open and back to the card on close.
- A card gets one real button (`button.shop-open`, transparent, over the whole card), named "<name>, <price>, show ingredients" ("... show what it is made of" for things that are not eaten) in the page's language; it is renamed when the language changes. The `<li>` stays a plain list item. The pizza bar's own two links stay above the button.
- Cards with a list: every product of groups 1-11 (the pizza and ice cream bars too: they have lists) and, once that group is merged, the 16 beer, cider and wine drinks. No list: the farm visit group, the five older cards (Farm fun x2, U-pick pumpkins, strawberries, blueberries), and the old "Beer, hard cider & wine" card (replaced by the beer group).

## Files
- `js/shop-sample.js`: the made-up lists, one IIFE, `window.WISE_ACRES.shopSample = { "<picture name>": { heading, list, contains } }`, 71 entries, first line "SAMPLE DATA, NOT REAL. English only. Must not go on the real website until the farm gives the real lists." Assembled from the data helpers' fragments by `/tmp/helpers/ingredients/assemble.py` (kept outside the repo). Without this file the whole feature is dormant.
- `js/shop-pop.js`: the small loader. When `shopSample` is on the page it asks for `js/shop-pop-box.js`; otherwise it does nothing (no button, no class, no request).
- `js/shop-pop-box.js`: the cards' buttons and classes, and the pop-up. It asks for `css/shop-pop.css` when a card is first pointed at, as `js/tour.js` asks for `css/tour.css` (the preview puts both in the page: see below).
- `css/shop-pop.css`: the pop-up's look. `css/extras.css`: a short hover block after the Store rules (inert without the script).
- `tests/shop-pop.test.mjs`, `lang/src/{es,hi,vi,zh}.json` (six new words each, under "js", placed after "Close"), `tools/pages.py` (the two script tags stay on the home page: the other pages have no Store), `tests/code-rules.test.mjs` and `tests/no-js.test.mjs` (one pattern each: the two lazily loaded files), `work-notes/preview/build-artifact.py` (one line).

## For a tour helper (later)
A card that has a list carries the class `shop-card-pop` and, inside it, `button.shop-open` (a click on it opens the pop-up; the pop-up is `div.shop-pop > div.shop-pop-box`, open while `.shop-pop` has `is-open`). The first snack, `#shop-snacks .shop-card-pop`, is a good card to light. The feature is only there when `js/shop-sample.js` is on the page, so a step about it must be left out when `WISE_ACRES.shopSample` is missing.

## Running the test
`flock /tmp/wa-heavy.lock node tests/shop-pop.test.mjs` (about 6 minutes on a quiet computer: it opens about 25 pages). To work on one part: `SHOPPOP_ONLY=hover` (or `dormant`, `data`, `keys`, `languages`, `bigfont`, `preview`, several with commas). The part "preview" builds the preview into a temporary folder and needs python3.

## The real site
The farm has not given real lists, so the real site should not carry the made-up ones. To leave the feature out of a push, drop `js/shop-sample.js` and its `<script>` line from `index.html`. The small `js/shop-pop.js` line may stay (it does nothing without the sample, and asks for nothing), or go too, with `js/shop-pop-box.js` and `css/shop-pop.css`. The hover block in `css/extras.css` matches nothing without the script and may stay. When the real lists arrive, replace `js/shop-sample.js` (same shape) and everything works.

## Browser-support table
Nothing new is needed: `node tests/browser-support.test.mjs` passes with these files. `inert` is set as an attribute next to `aria-hidden` (`setAttribute('inert', '')`), so it is not a name the table lists: a browser that does not know it still has `aria-hidden` and the Tab loop. `:focus-visible` is asked in a `try`, and the CSS uses only properties the site already uses (`touch-action`, `place-items`, `position: sticky`, `min()`, custom properties).

## What the preview build must do
One line in `work-notes/preview/build-artifact.py` (added here, in `script_block`, just before `return block`): for `js/shop-pop.js` it puts `css/shop-pop.css` in the page as `<style data-shop-pop-css>` and `js/shop-pop-box.js` in a `<script>` in front of it, so that nothing is asked for later (the viewer lets a page load only its own published files). `js/shop-pop.js` and `js/shop-sample.js` are plain `<script>` tags of `index.html`, so the build inlines them in place already.

## Things I was not sure about (the default I took)
- Decision 1 of the spec says the `.shop-full` bars have no entry, but the data helper's spec and test 2 of the spec cover groups 2 and 3, so the pizza and ice cream bars HAVE lists and a button. They grow less (4.5 percent) and their own links stay on top of the button.
- The spec says `js/shop-pop.js` is the loader plus the pop-up code. To keep the real site's first visit light (the real file set is at 415 KB of the 420 KB limit), the pop-up code is in a second file, `js/shop-pop-box.js`, that the small `js/shop-pop.js` asks for only when the sample lists are there. Folding the two files together is a copy and paste; the preview build line and the two test patterns would go back to one file.
- The button covers the whole card, so the card's words cannot be selected with the mouse. Links in a card (the pizza bar's two) stay above it.
- Chinese: the notice keeps its parentheses as the full-width ones used in Chinese text.
- The beer group is not in this branch: the test accepts the page with or without it, and wants the 16 drinks to have lists once their cards are there.
