## The wish, turned into a design (you may improve it, but keep every point)
A. PRETEND-LIVE FLOW (both screens). A "pretend rush" that keeps producing believable incoming things while the screen is open, so Miss Cathy sees how the real thing would feel: family requests (change a day, a question, a photo/review waiting) arriving at the desk; pizza orders and pizza change requests arriving at the kitchen. A clear control in the demo bar or on each screen: "Pretend rush: On / Off" and speed (Calm / Busy / Rush). Default: ON when a staff screen is opened in the demo? (decide; it must be obvious and easy to pause; the tour and tests need a way to turn it off or to run it with a fake clock). The flow uses the shared pretend store so the three screens stay consistent: an order that arrives shows on the kitchen, a family request shows on the desk, answers show on the family's account when the pretend family is the one signed in. The flow must be DETERMINISTIC with a seed and a clock the tests can fake (no real randomness in tests), must not grow without bound (cap and recycle old items), and must never start by itself in a real visitor's browser (demo flags only).
B. PIZZA KITCHEN REDESIGN: a kitchen-display screen for a busy kitchen: look, decide, keep rolling. Big type, high contrast, readable from 2 metres on a tablet or a computer; tickets in lanes (for example New, Needs an answer, Making, Ready) or one sorted rail; each ticket: big order number, pretend family name, the pizzas big, pickup time, notes, how long it waited, and an "answer by" timer. Answering must take ONE press or key: the likely answers are big buttons (for example Yes / No / Another time) with keyboard keys (1, 2, 3 or Y, N, S) and arrow keys to move between tickets; after an answer the next ticket that needs one is focused automatically ("keep rolling"). Editing an answer: a small inline editor (change the pickup time with +5 / +10 / +15 minute buttons or the keyboard, change a note) and ENTER sends it, ESC cancels. Works with touch (big targets, no hover-only things), keyboard only and a screen reader (aria-live announces a new ticket, a late ticket, an answer).
C. TIMER AND BEEPING: every ticket that needs an answer has an answer-by countdown (pretend minutes; default 2:00 for a change request, 3:00 for a new order, adjustable in a small settings strip), shown as a bar or ring, green then amber then red; when time runs out the ticket turns red and stays on top ("late") and, when sound is switched on, it beeps again and again (gentle, not harsh) until answered; a new ticket gets one soft chime. Sound is OFF until a person presses "Sound on" (browsers block sound before a press; also respect reduced motion and keep a visible mute). Counters at the top: waiting, answered, late; and a small clock. Do not make anything flash faster than 3 times a second.
D. DESK (Mrs. Cathy and Rob): the flow of pretend family requests, questions, photos and reviews with a live feed ("just now: ..."), counters (waiting / answered today), and the existing answer buttons working while new items keep coming; calm enough to read; the same pretend-rush control; no beeping needed on the desk (a quiet badge/toast is enough) unless it fits.
E. TOUR: update/add chapter 10 cards (js/tour-steps-c.js, test tests/tour-demo-script.test.mjs and tests/tour-targets.test.mjs chapter 10) so that Miss Cathy is walked through: the pretend rush (what it is, how to start/pause it), the new kitchen screen (tickets, one-press answers, Enter to send an edit, the timer, what late means, the beep and the Sound button), and the desk's live flow. Each card <= 45 words, plain words, true, with lit parts that contain the quoted words. Do not break the existing cards' order logic or their `before()` functions that move the pretend store forward.
F. DOCS: docs/ACCOUNT_DEMO.md (how to use the new screens), work-notes/NOTES-<yourname>.md (what you did, README text for main to put in tests/README.md), docs/TOUR.md only if you change the engine.
