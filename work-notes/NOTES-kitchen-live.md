## What the builder asked, and what is on the screen
"Pretend data flowing" for the desk and the pizza section, and a pizza section "very good for a busy pizza kitchen to just look at and then choose an answer and then keep rolling, or edit the answer and click enter, pretend live, with a timer where they need to answer at a certain time or it keeps beeping."

- **`js/demo-live.js`** (new): the pretend rush, the engine both screens use (`window.DemoLive`). Seeded, deterministic, kept in the shared store, never starts by itself, bounded. The API note is the comment at the top of the file; `docs/ACCOUNT_DEMO.md` has "The pretend rush" for editors.
- **`js/demo-kitchen.js`, `css/demo-kitchen.css`**: the kitchen as a display. One list "Needs an answer" (late tickets on top, then the soonest deadline), side lanes "With the family" and "Done", big order number, family, pizzas, pick-up, notes, "Waited m:ss", an answer-by timer (green, amber, red, "Late m:ss"), three big answer buttons with their keys (1/Y, 2/N, 3/S; a new order has no No), up/down arrows, the next ticket that needs an answer gets the focus, a small inline editor ("Another time": -5 +5 +10 +15, the plus and minus keys, a note; Enter sends, Esc closes), the Sound button (off until pressed; a soft chime for arrivals, a gentle beep when a ticket turns late and every 7 s while any is late, none while paused; M mutes), counters (Waiting, Answered, Late), a pretend clock, the answer-by settings under the board, a polite status line, `role="timer"` timers that do not chatter, reduced motion, touch sizes.
- **`js/demo-store.js`** (small): the pretend families' records (`live: true`, `fam`) are hidden from `list`, `openRequests`, `threadsWaiting` unless `{ live: true }` is passed; a live record never makes a notice; `decide` stamps `decided`; `transact(fn)`; the object `live`; a new sample keeps the rush's choices and forgets its families.
- **`js/tour-steps-c.js`**: six kitchen cards on the free numbers 1041 to 1046 (rush control, a ticket, the timer, one-press answers, the small editor, the sound), written as what the screen does (the builder's rule of 10 Nov). The kitchen story card (`ch10-kitchen`, 1044) keeps its id and its job.
- **`pages/kitchen.html`**: `js/demo-live.js` after `js/demo-ui.js`. `js/demo-pizza-editor.js` is still loaded (the My pizza screen shares it); the kitchen no longer uses it (see "Decisions").

## Decisions I took (the spec left them open)
1. **Default OFF**, with a big invitation on both screens (agreed with desk-live): the existing tests and the tour keep their states, and the control is obvious. The choice is kept in the one demo key; Start over forgets it.
2. **Off is a pause**: no arrivals and the pretend clock, the timers and the beeps stand still (the screen says "paused"). A page that was closed for hours does not age the pretend world (at most 8 s per write), so no ticket is suddenly late.
3. **The pretend families are in the normal lists, hidden by default** (desk-live asked for this and it is the one way the existing answer buttons work on them). Ids have an L so the family's own ids never change.
4. **A ticket of the signed-in family starts its timer when the kitchen first sees it** (`DemoLive.watch`, kept in the store, so a reload does not restart it).
5. **No Another-time-for-pizzas editor any more**: the old "Change it" opened the shared pizza editor (pizzas, toppings, crust). The new "Another time" changes the pick-up time and a note, as the builder described ("edit the answer and click enter"). A suggestion keeps the family's asked pizzas and offers a new time. If Mrs. Cathy wants pizza edits in the kitchen back, the shared editor (`DemoPizza.editor`, mode `kitchen`) can open from the small editor.
6. A new order can be confirmed (Yes) or moved (Another time: confirmed for the new time, the family is told both times). There is no "No" for a new order, because the family's My pizza screen has no state for a refused order.
7. The Answered counter is kept by the engine (`tally`), not counted from the records, so it never shrinks when old pretend tickets are tidied away.
8. Late tickets are sorted to the top only when a ticket turns late or something arrives or is answered (the DOM order is not changed every second), so nothing moves under a finger.
9. A pretend family answers a kitchen suggestion by itself 5 to 18 rush seconds later (OK in about 6 of 7), also on the desk (the engine does it inside its own write, so every tab agrees).

## Not sure, and what I did
- Sound can only be tried by ear: the chime is two sine notes (659 and 880 Hz, 0.2 s each, volume 0.05 to 0.06), the late beep one note (523 Hz, 0.3 s, 0.05). Quiet on purpose; if it is too soft in a real kitchen, change the volumes in `makeSound()`.
- The kitchen display shows the signed-in family's ticket under the name "Alex's family" (the demo family is `Alex`), number 101 (100 plus the order's own number).
- Timers for the sample family's ticket count in pretend time, so they only run while the rush is On. With the rush Off a ticket shows "2:00 left" and "paused (the pretend rush is off)". That keeps the tour's cards and every test still.
- The pretend farm clock and the real clock differ outside the farm's hours (the pretend clock starts at 11:00 am then). The kitchen shows the pretend one once the rush has run; before that, the real farm clock.
- Pressing a key while another tab has already answered the same ticket: the answer says so politely and changes nothing (the check compares the request with the status the answer would make).
