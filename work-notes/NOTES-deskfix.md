## (c) The pretend week and the open days: not small
"This week" lists today (a Tuesday: "Not a farm day") with pretend people and pizza pick-ups, and pretend visits on Thursday to Sunday, while the site's banner says the farm is open only on the tree weekends in winter, without a reservation. Two causes: the engine dates a pretend order today whatever the day is (`L.baseDate`), and the whole demo's open-day rule is `DemoData.openDays` (Thursday to Sunday for a farm visit, Friday to Sunday with pizza), which does not know the seasons; the same rule feeds the family's Visits screen, the store, the desk's weather form and the engine. A fix that is only on the desk or only in the engine would make the screens disagree. What it would take: (1) one rule for the open days that reads the site's own `js/season.js` (`treeWeekends(year)`: in winter only those days, no reservation, so the demo's booking, "pizza with a visit" and "close a day" need a winter version), in `js/demo-data.js` (not mine); (2) the engine takes its pretend dates (the visit-change days, the date of an order) from it and moves its pretend day to the next open day when today is closed (the kitchen and the desk would then say that day, not "today"); (3) `isFarmDay` and the This week row of the desk use the same rule; (4) tests: demo-data (openDays by season), demo-store, the visits and booking tests of the family screens, demo-live, demo-desk-live, kitchen-live. About a day of work across four helpers' files; I did not start it.
