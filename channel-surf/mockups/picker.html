Things to try in each one

  1. Press the big power button, or any button on the remote.
  2. Flip with the remote's CH ▲▼, or the ↑ ↓ keys.
  3. Type a channel number: 5, or 4 then OK. Press LAST (Backspace) to jump back.
  4. Press GUIDE (G). Move around with the arrows, press OK to watch.
  5. Press MENU (Home), then Settings, then turn on Bigger text.
  6. Tune to channel 9, which has no shows, to see the "off the air" screen.
  7. Use the switches at the top: turn Static off, or hide the Remote to see the screen bigger. Try Full screen too.

Why does the picture shrink when the banner shows? YouTube's rules don't allow anything drawn on top of its player. So instead of covering the show, the picture briefly makes room, then goes back to full screen.

Each look does that a different way, which is part of what you're choosing.

Sound effects (static, clicks, chimes) start after your first click.
