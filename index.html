    Questions? We’ve got answers

    The quick version. Need more? Call us or send a note — we’re happy to help.

    Read the full FAQ
    Do I need a reservation?

    Yes. Wise Acres is reservation-only and we can’t accommodate walk-ins. Spots — especially strawberry season and peak weekends — fill up quickly, so reserve as early as you can.

    Where is the farm?

    Wise Acres Organic Farm is at 4701 Hartis Road, Indian Trail, NC 28079, just outside Charlotte. The GreenHouse is a short hop away at 5503 Poplin Rd.

    Is the farm really organic?

    Yes! Wise Acres is a USDA Certified Organic, family-owned farm, growing with natural methods.

    What’s included with my reservation?

    Your reservation gets you into the u-pick fields (strawberries, blueberries, pumpkins and flowers in season), the animals, playgrounds, scavenger hunt and shaded picnic areas. On fall visits there’s a small maze, too. Wagon rides and the corn pit are available as add-ons.

    Can I get pizza with my visit?

    Yes — choose a reservation with Wise Pie wood-fired pizza or one without. Pizza reservations open every Tuesday at 5:00 PM for the upcoming weekend.

    When is each season?

    Strawberries usually run mid-April to early June, blueberries and sunflowers June to early July, and pumpkins mid-September to early November. Exact dates depend on the weather.

    Who do I contact with questions?

    Call (704) 628-6232 or email cathy@wiseacresorganic.com.
