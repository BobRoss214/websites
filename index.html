    First-visit guide

    Your first visit to Wise Acres

    Everything to know before you come: how reservations work, what to bring and what to expect when you arrive.

    • Reserve a time

      The farm is always reservation-only. Book online and pick a time. When new reservations open, many people use the site at once, so it can show a temporary error. That does not mean we are full. Please try again after the first busy period.

      Reserve now

    • Changes and rain

      We’re farmers, so a little rain doesn’t close the farm.

      • You can change your reservation at no charge until 11:59 PM the night before your visit
      • If you cancel by then, we refund the cost of your reservation, minus the 3% credit card processing fee
      • If severe weather forces us to close, we’ll email you and give a full refund

    • Paying

      Cash is preferred. We also accept all major credit cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay.

    • When you arrive

      We’re at 4701 Hartis Road in Indian Trail, in a neighborhood with limited parking. Check in under the name on your reservation.

      • No reservation? The GreenHouse next door at 5503 Poplin Rd welcomes drop-ins.

    • What to bring

      • Your reservation confirmation (email or phone)
      • Cash, a card, Apple Pay or Google Pay
      • Water and a snack for the shady picnic spots
      • Shoes that can get dirty
      • Sunscreen and a hat
      • A cooler bag for berries on warm days

    • Dogs

      Unfortunately, pets must stay home because of liability and food-safety regulations. We love dogs! Service animals are welcome.

    • Bathrooms

      We offer portable toilets (porta-johns) and portable hand-washing stations with foot pumps throughout the farm. One toilet is accessible for people with disabilities. Our farm uses well water and a septic system, so portable facilities are our only option for now.

    • Strollers & wheelchairs

      Wise Acres is a working farm. Irrigation lines, support stakes and farm equipment may be in the fields. If you have questions about getting around with a stroller or wheelchair, please email us before you come.

      Email us

    • Parking

      We are in a neighborhood on Hartis Road with limited parking. Vehicles are not allowed in the fields, barn or garden areas.

    Why don’t you post pizza dates far ahead?

    Each season we apply for county permits. The county only approves a limited number of days and hours, so we cannot always post menus and hours far ahead of time. Schedules may sometimes change, and on busy weekends we may be sold out.

    Thank you for your understanding as we work hard to follow all local rules and regulations.

    Who we are

    It started with our kids

    The way Wise Acres got its start may be a little unconventional, but the reason behind it is simple: our kids.

    Bailey, Morgan and Mac inspired us to grow the healthiest, most delicious berries we possibly can. We left our hometown of Chicago and moved our family to beautiful North Carolina. In March of 2013 we started work on the farm, a dream we had talked about for years.

    Before farming, we worked in finance and business. We really wanted a place where our kids could enjoy the same opportunities we had growing up. We wanted it to have the space, fresh air and outdoor life we all love. After a long search, we found the perfect piece of land in Indian Trail.

    When we first stepped onto the property, we quickly saw that we had a lot of work to do. So we got right to work. We have big plans for the future of the farm. We are grateful for the amazing customers who have helped Wise Acres grow along the way.

    Our berries

    Our family lives on the farm, and we eat the berries we grow, so we care deeply about how they are grown. We grow our strawberries without the poisons commonly used in conventional strawberry farming. Many of those chemicals do not stay only in the field. They can also end up in the fruit, the soil and the water we share with our neighbors.

    Strawberries regularly rank at the top of the Environmental Working Group’s “Dirty Dozen” list for pesticide contamination. That’s one more reason we grow berries in a way that’s healthier for our family, our customers and our community.

    Organic

    Wise Acres is a USDA Certified Organic farm. We rotate our crops, plant cover crops and keep detailed records. Outside inspectors check us regularly to make sure we meet all current organic standards.

    These practices help us care for our soil, protect the environment and grow berries you can feel good about eating. We are committed to providing the healthiest berries possible.

    Local

    Most produce in supermarkets is harvested 5–7 days before it reaches the shelves. During that time, natural sugars begin turning to starch, and fruit gradually loses flavor and nutrients. Many grocery-store varieties are chosen because they ship well, not because they taste best.

    On average, food travels about 1,500 miles before reaching the supermarket. When you buy local, you get produce that was picked fresh and is ready to eat, and you support lower energy use and less packaging.

    Care & handling

    We do our very best to grow the sweetest, most flavorful berries possible. Once you leave the farm, a few simple steps keep them at their best.

    1. Refrigerate your berries as soon as you get home.
    2. Don’t wash them until you’re ready to serve.
    3. Rinse gently under cool water with the green caps still attached.
    4. Let them warm to room temperature before serving for the best flavor.

          Ask a question
          When are you open?

          Our seasons vary each year with weather and crop conditions.

          • Spring, strawberries: usually mid-April through early June
          • Summer, blueberries & sunflowers: usually mid- to late June through early July
          • Fall, pumpkins: mid-September through early November
          • Fall, tomatoes & basil (new this year): every weekend, late September through October
          • Winter, NC Christmas trees: the Friday after Thanksgiving to early December, at The GreenHouse
          • U-cut flowers: April–July and September–first frost

          Exact opening dates depend on the weather. We share updates as crops begin to ripen, so join our email list.

          Can I visit the farm without a reservation?

          No. We are open by reservation only. Please don’t come to the farm without one.

          Wise Acres is a small family farm in a neighborhood with limited parking. When we’re not open to the public, we’re working in the fields. Reservations let us manage parking, protect the crops and give guests, neighbors and farm families a safe, enjoyable experience.

          We’re the only USDA-certified organic strawberry farm in the area, and we grow far fewer berries than conventional farms. Organic practices mean we plant only about one-third of our fields at a time, so supply is limited. In the past, too much picking damaged the crops, and the farm lost income. Reservations help us protect the farm while giving visitors the best experience. Thank you for your patience and support.

          Want to visit without a reservation? Go to our property next door, The GreenHouse at 5503 Poplin Rd. No reservation is required, and hours vary with the weather.

          When do reservations open?

          Fall: reservations without pizza open a month or more at a time. Reservations with pizza open every Tuesday at 5:00 PM for the upcoming weekend. See the current fall schedule.

          Often, when reservations first open, so many people use the site at once that it stops working well and shows an error message. That does not mean reservations are full. Please check back after the first busy period.

          Spring: reservations open when we start to see fruit ripen, usually a few weeks before the season starts. Strawberry season is our busiest time, and reservations fill quickly. The amount of fruit rises to a peak and then falls. Peak picking is usually in late April through early May. At the start of the season we open only a small number of reservations. Then we usually open more dates each week, while we watch the field conditions and the weather.

          In other seasons we can usually have more visitors. But parking limits, county permits, staffing and the weather still affect how many spots are open.

          How can I enjoy Wise Pie pizza?

          Each season the county limits the number of cooking-permit days and hours we are allowed. We do not post dates until the county approves our permits.

          At the farm, until 4:00 PM: pizza is only with a farm reservation.
          At The GreenHouse, Friday–Sunday, 4:00–8:00 PM: no reservation is required, but there is no farm access. This is an easy choice for neighbors, and for anyone who only wants one pizza.

          More details in the Wise Pie section.

          Do you have u-pick tomatoes and basil?

          Yes, and it’s new this year! Certified organic cherry tomatoes, slicers and basil are available to u-pick every weekend from late September through October. Tomatoes are $4.50 per pound and basil is $1 per stem.

          See every variety in the tomato & basil guide.

          Where can I get a Christmas tree?

          Christmas trees are at The GreenHouse (5503 Poplin Rd). Tree season starts the Friday after Thanksgiving and runs to early December. No reservation is needed at The GreenHouse.

          Are dogs allowed?

          Unfortunately, no. We love dogs, but because of liability and food-safety regulations, pets must stay home. Service animals are welcome.

          What forms of payment do you accept?

          Cash is preferred. We also accept all major credit cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay.

          What is your rain policy?

          We’re farmers, so a little rain doesn’t close the farm.

          • You can change your reservation at no charge until 11:59 PM the night before your visit
          • If you cancel by then, we refund the cost of your reservation, minus the 3% credit card processing fee
          • If severe weather forces us to close, we’ll email you and give a full refund
          How do I change or cancel my reservation?

          Open your confirmation or reminder email and click the “View Booking” button at the bottom. It takes you straight to your reservation page, where you can make changes.

          You can give your reservation to someone else. That person can simply check in under the reservation name.

          What are your bathroom facilities?

          We currently have portable toilets (porta-johns) and portable hand-washing stations with foot pumps throughout the farm. One toilet is accessible for people with disabilities.

          Our farm uses well water and a septic system. These are not approved for commercial use. For over a decade we have asked for city water and sewer, but our requests were denied. Until that changes, portable facilities are our only option. Thank you for your understanding.

          Where do I park?

          We are in a neighborhood on Hartis Road with limited parking. Vehicles are not allowed in the fields, barn or garden areas.
