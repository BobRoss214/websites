          Fair by design

          Equal odds

          Every charity in play has exactly the same chance, in every game. Even Plinko, where a real peg board would favour the middle.

          Secure randomness

          Winners come from your browser's cryptographic random generator, with no modulo bias. The animation reveals a result that is already decided.

          Nothing hidden

          The wheel tells you how many charities are in play, and every round ends with a clear breakdown of who got what, to the cent.

          FAQ

          Good questions.

          Is this real money?

          Not yet. This site is running in demo mode: rounds are simulated from start to finish, nothing is charged, and no donation is made. Every receipt is stamped DEMO so nobody is misled. The site owner can connect real checkout in js/config.js (see the README).

          Yes. When a game picks a charity you finish your gift on a checkout page run by the donation platform. GiveSpin never sees or stores your card details.

          How is the winner picked?

          By your browser's cryptographic random number generator (crypto.getRandomValues), using rejection sampling so no charity is favoured. The result is chosen first and the game animates to it. When the pool is bigger than the wheel, a random subset is shown and the winner is drawn from it, which is still equal odds for the whole pool.

          Can I choose which charities can win?

          Yes, two ways. Tap cause chips (Kids, Animals, Planet…) to narrow the pool, or open the roster and switch individual charities off.

          What does "split your gift" do?

          It divides your amount into equal parts, to the cent, and plays one round per part. Give $10 across 3 rounds and you get $3.34, $3.33 and $3.33, each going to whichever charity that round lands on. Slots always use three reels, so a pull is automatically a three-way split.

          Are there fees or tax receipts?

          GiveSpin adds no fee of its own. Any processing fees are set by the checkout provider and shown before you pay. Tax receipts come from the charity or provider that processes the gift, not from GiveSpin, and demo receipts are not tax documents.

          Can I use this on stream?

          That is what it is built for. Hit the TV button at the top for Stream Mode (or add ?stream=1 to the URL). It enlarges the game and hides everything else. Add &transparent=1 for a see-through background in an OBS browser source. Space bar plays.

          What do you store about me?

          Nothing leaves your browser. Your level, streak, badges and history are saved in this browser's local storage so they are still there next visit, and you can wipe them any time from the Impact section. There are no accounts, trackers or ads.
