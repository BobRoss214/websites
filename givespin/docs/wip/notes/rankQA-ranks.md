# rankQA hand-off: independent quality check of the rank system

Scope: the merged rank system (`core.js` ranks, `js/ranks.js`, `js/ui/rankui.js`, `css/ranks.css`, `css/rankui.css`, every place that shows a chip, and tests 13zr).
Base = the merged tree as I received it (`base/givespin`). The whole patch is `changes.patch` (9 files, 238 lines). All paths below are relative to `givespin/`.

Short version: the rank rules (boundaries, levels inside a rank, whole cents, old/odd saves, nothing stored) are solid and I found no logic error in them. The emblems are good:
eight distinct designs that get cooler rank by rank, and they stay distinct at 16 and 24 px even in greyscale and colour-blind simulations. I found and fixed 7 real defects,
all small (one of them is "the two top emblems could never be seen on a bot"). 2 findings are left for the lead (see "Found and not fixed").

## 2. Found and NOT fixed (for the lead)

1. **`js/ui/live.js` line 658 (outside my territory, so I did not touch it).** `levelNote()`: for a ranked player whose *XP* level went up it prints " · Level up!" with no qualifier, while everywhere else (receipt) that case is "XP level up! Your XP level is now ...". It can only happen with an old or hand-edited save that has $1,000+ given but under 9,000 XP (normal play gives 10 XP per $1, so a ranked player always has 10,000+ XP), so it is cosmetic and rare. Suggested one-line fix: `return sm.leveledUp ? (sm.rankAfter.rank ? ' · XP level up!' : ' · Level up!') : '';`
2. **A rank-up is celebrated by a stack of sounds.** At a live table that wins: the result's win sound and confetti, then 0.15 s later the rank-up jingle, then 0.4 s after that a second win sound (3 jingles inside about 0.6 s, one confetti burst plus the rank-up's burst and shower). For a solo gift the same, spread over 1.7 s after the receipt. It is *one* rank-up dialog per result (verified: a single `dialog open`, and the existing `told` guard holds when `resultToast` and `celebrate` both report the same result), and 13zr asserts the fanfare (`levelUp === 1 && win >= 2`) as intended, so I treated it as a design decision and left it. If it is too much in play, drop the `setTimeout(... GS.audio.win ...)` in `rankui.js` `moment()` (and change that one assertion).
3. Smaller notes, no action needed: `core.rankFor(Infinity)` is "nothing given" while `1e300` is rank 8 (a test asserts it; `store` can never hold Infinity). The pinned Immortal handle (CharityCat) shows in about 40% of 24-row feeds (rare per row, not per table; change the pin or remove it if the lead wants it rarer). At a forced 200% font size on a 1280 px window the Giving Club's stat tile for a $1,900,000 total overflows by 63 px; that is a long dollar number in a tile, nothing to do with ranks, and a real 200% browser zoom (640 css px) does not overflow.

## 3. Checked and fine (what I did, with numbers)

**Logic (node + browser):**
- Boundaries, via `core` and via saves in the browser: $999.99 = "Level 10", $1,000.00 = Rank 1 Level 1, $4,999.99 / $5,000, $999,999.99 = R7 L10, $1,000,000.00 = R8 L1, $1,900,000 and above = R8 L10 "maxed", 1e15 and 1e300 cents are capped at R8 L10 with no error anywhere; fractions of a cent floor (99999.7 is not ranked); negative, `null`, a numeric string and NaN totals read as nothing given. The unit tests already pin all of this; I re-ran it as saves through the whole UI (`tools/odd.mjs`: 9 odd saves x 6 pages + side card, 0 console/page errors).
- Old save, lots of XP and little given ($5 given, 123,456 XP): "Level 10", goal "Next rank: Patron". Old save with $7,500 given and 40 XP: Rank 2 · Level 6 with the XP ladder keeping its own place. A save that contains `rank`, `level`, `standing` fields is ignored; the saved JSON never has a rank in it (checked in the browser after a rank-up).
- Receipt vs live vs league vs club wording: "Rank up!" only for a new rank, "Level up!" for a level inside a rank (or the old XP level before $1,000), "XP level up!" for an XP level of a ranked player. One exception, see "not fixed" 1.
- A single gift that jumps ranks (rankedUp with `rankBefore` 2 ranks lower): one dialog that ends on the final rank and says "you jumped ahead".
- Double celebration: a solo gift and a live result each open exactly one rank-up dialog (instrumented `dialog[open]` count, `audio.levelUp` count 1, `confetti`/`shower` calls logged: `tools/livecross.mjs`, `tools/solophone.mjs`). Focus goes to "Keep giving"; closing returns focus to where it was (the receipt's "Play again", or the live tab); Esc closes only the rank-up dialog and leaves the receipt open.
- Nothing stored that should not be (see above); `GS.ranks` missing -> chips still read right (the existing 13zr check).

**Emblems (`shots/emblems-*.png`):**
- Rendered all 8 at 16, 24, 40, 96, 176 px on the dark theme at 1x and 2x pixel ratio, plus 18/32 px. They read as eight distinct designs and get cooler in order: plain bronze shield with a heart, silver shield with a star, gold winged shield with a star and a shine, emerald winged shield with a gem heart, sapphire laurel wreath with a crown, ruby sunburst medallion, obsidian crest with lightning, a rainbow ring with a crown and a flame.
- 16 and 24 px stay distinct by **silhouette**, not hue: tested in greyscale and in deuteranopia, protanopia and tritanopia simulations (`shots/emblems-colour-blind-sim-16-and-24px.png`, matrices of Machado et al.). The only close pair in greyscale at 16 px is Benefactor (star on a notched shield) vs Champion (star on a shield with wing stubs); the chip's "R2" / "R3" text separates them as well. No clipped paths at any size (nothing touches the viewBox edge; chips set `overflow: visible` for the glow).
- Big sizes have no ugly gradients or banding; the ring of ten level segments is legible at 112 px (lit vs dim) and at 40 px.
- Animation: at 40 px and under there is none; 41 px and up only without `prefers-reduced-motion`. Under reduced motion `getComputedStyle(...).animationName` is `none` for all 50 animated parts on the Giving Club at rank 8, and the rank-up dialog's glow and pop are off (`tools/rm.mjs`). Frame times on the Club page at rank 8 with all 9 animated emblems: avg 16.6 ms, max 17 ms, 0 frames over 33 ms (headless Chromium).
