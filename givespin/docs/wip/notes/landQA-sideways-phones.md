## NOT fixed / known limits (decide whether to act)
1. **axe `target-size` on the scrolling landscape panel (844x390: 14 of 19 games; 568x320: Dice; 1280x600: 10 games with "Choose a charity").** Axe flags whichever control sits across the scroll box's bottom edge at rest ("partially obscured" by the Play button): it looks at boxes and ignores that the rest is just scrolled out of sight. Scrolled to a spot where nothing straddles the edge it reports nothing (1280x600: 0). The same would happen for any scrolling list. I did not add axe to 13zt for this reason (it would fail on a geometry artefact); I did not find a CSS-only fix that keeps the panel scrolling.
2. **Slot machines at 568x320** are drawn at about 0.5, so Turbo and "How it pays" are 17.8 px tall (axe: target-size, real). Play does the main job from the Play bar. Fixing needs bigger native buttons (they would shrink the machine again) or moving those buttons out of the scaled picture.
3. **Windows narrower than 561 px and shorter than about 520 px (e.g. 480x320, 540x300)** use the docked layout (the "any window narrower than 561 px" rule), and no picture can fit there: dock + nav bar + top bar leave nothing. 568x320 (iPhone SE first generation) is the smallest sideways phone covered. Making the landscape layout start lower than 561 px would be a design decision.
4. Pictures are still small at 568x320 for the race games and Balloon Race (0.36 to 0.5); that is the scale floor the owner accepted. Plinko bin names and Duck Derby names are small there.
5. With the sheet open, `fitStage()` does not refit (by design: the sheet only covers the picture). If the window is turned while the sheet is open, the picture keeps the old size until the sheet closes (then it refits; tested). Not visible, so left alone.
6. The Pick a Card winner card zooms past the top of its row on 568x320 (existing animation, cosmetic).
