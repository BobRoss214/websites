# Charities still to add to the roster

The roster is at 381 charities. The target is 1,000. Every charity added so far was checked with web searches (it exists, its official
website, and its founding year and headquarters where the results stated them); the evidence links are in `roster-sources.json`.

The checking stopped because the web-search allowance for the session (200 searches) ran out, and the charities' own sites and the
charity evaluators could not be fetched from that environment. Nothing below has been checked yet, so none of it is in the app.
These are names to verify (existence, official domain, founding year, headquarters) before adding them. Do not copy a website
address from here: look it up.

How to continue: raise the search allowance (`CLAUDE_CODE_MAX_WEB_SEARCHES_PER_SESSION`) or allow fetching charity-evaluator and
Wikipedia domains, then re-run the research with the brief in this repository's history (one search per charity, `unverified` if a fact
cannot be confirmed). `tests/data.test.js` enforces the entry format.

## Leave out (found while researching)
- Anything with misuse-of-funds findings or scam warnings (for example Operation Underground Railroad, Narconon and the Foundation for a Drug-Free World).
- White Ribbon Australia (went into administration in 2023).
- Bodies that are not donation-eligible charities in the usual sense: Alcoholics Anonymous, Al-Anon, Narcotics Anonymous; 501(c)(4) and (c)(19) organisations such as the VFW, AMVETS, Paralyzed Veterans of America, the ACLU.
- Islamic Relief USA: its name and domain were changing in September 2026. Check the current status before adding.
- Programmes of a larger body (for example UMCOR, which is part of the General Board of Global Ministries) and sub-brands of charities already listed.
