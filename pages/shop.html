Pumpkins

Pick your perfect pumpkin from a variety of types in our patch, and take home NC pumpkins from the farm.

  • U-pick pumpkins in a variety of types
  • Pumpkin season is mid-September through early November
  • The farm is open Thursday–Sunday by reservation
  • Wagon rides, a corn pit and a flower field to enjoy while you’re here
