Pizza questions

Before you order

Where can I get Wise Pie pizza?

At the farm until 4:00 PM with a farm reservation package that includes pizza, and at The GreenHouse (5503 Poplin Rd) Friday through Sunday from 4:00 to 8:00 PM with no reservation required.

Do I need a reservation for pizza?

Not at The GreenHouse, where pizza is first come, first served from 4 to 8 pm. At the farm, pizza is available only with a pre-purchased farm reservation package.

Is there a vegan or gluten-free option?

Yes. Follow Your Heart vegan cheese and Wholly Wholesome gluten-free crust are available for an extra charge.

How big are the pizzas?

Each pizza is 12 inches and sold as a whole pie only. One pizza serves about 2 to 3 adults or 3 to 4 children.

Why don’t you post pizza dates far ahead?

Each season we apply for county permits. The county allows a limited number of approved days and hours, so we can’t always post menus and hours far in advance. Schedules may occasionally change, and on busy weekends we may sell out.
