Your first visit to Wise Acres

Everything to know before you come: how reservations work, what to bring and what to expect when you arrive.

Reserve your time Directions

Step one

Before you come

  • Reserve a time

    The farm is always reservation-only. Book online and pick a time. When a new batch opens, heavy traffic can show a temporary error. That does not mean we’re full, so try again after the first rush.

    Reserve now

  • Changes and rain

    You can change your reservation at no charge until 11:59 PM the night before your visit. We’re farmers, so a little rain doesn’t close the farm. If severe weather forces us to close, we’ll email you and give a full refund.

  • Bringing friends

    Reservations are transferable. The person attending can simply check in under the reservation name. To make changes, open your confirmation email and click “View Booking” at the bottom.

  • Paying

    Cash is preferred. We also accept all major credit cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay.

Step two

When you arrive

We’re at 4701 Hartis Road in Indian Trail, in a neighborhood with limited parking. Check in under the name on your reservation.

  • Vehicles are not allowed in the fields, barn or garden areas.
  • No reservation? The GreenHouse next door at 5503 Poplin Rd welcomes drop-ins.

Step three

Good to know

  • Dogs

    Unfortunately, pets must stay home because of liability and food-safety regulations. We love dogs! Service animals are welcome.

  • Bathrooms

    We offer porta-johns, including a handicapped-accessible unit, and portable foot-pump hand-washing stations throughout the farm. Our farm uses well water and a septic system, so portable facilities are our only option for now.

  • A working farm

    Wise Acres is a working farm. We take extra precautions to keep it safe, but the farm is not childproof. Irrigation lines, support stakes and farm equipment may be present throughout the fields.

Quick answers

First-timer questions

All FAQs
Can I visit the farm without a reservation?

No. We are open by reservation only. Please don’t come to the farm without one.

Wise Acres is a small family farm in a neighborhood with limited parking. Reservations let us manage parking, protect the crops and give guests, neighbors and farm families a safe, enjoyable experience.

Want to visit without a reservation? Stop by our adjacent property, The GreenHouse at 5503 Poplin Rd. No reservation is required, and hours vary with the weather.

What is your rain policy?

We’re farmers, so a little rain doesn’t close the farm.

  • You can change your reservation at no charge until 11:59 PM the night before your visit
  • If you cancel by then, we refund your reservation minus the 3% credit card processing fee
  • If severe weather forces us to close, we’ll email you and give a full refund
Are dogs allowed?

Unfortunately, no. We love dogs, but because of liability and food-safety regulations, pets must stay home. Service animals are welcome.

What are your bathroom facilities?

We currently offer porta-johns, including a handicapped-accessible unit, and portable foot-pump hand-washing stations throughout the farm.
