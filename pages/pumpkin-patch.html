Plan your trip

Good to know before you go

  • Reservations only

    The farm is always reservation-only, and we don’t take walk-ins. We’re in a neighborhood with limited parking, so booking a time keeps things safe and calm.

  • No reservation?

    Stop by The GreenHouse next door at 5503 Poplin Rd. No reservation is needed, and there’s a playground, goats, pizza and ice cream. It does not include farm access.

    Read our first-visit guide for parking, check-in, what to bring, bathrooms and the rain policy.

Pumpkin patch questions

Good answers for pumpkin season

When is pumpkin season at Wise Acres?

Pumpkin picking usually runs from mid-September through early November. Exact dates depend on the weather, so check the fall schedule or join our email list for updates.

Do I need a reservation for the pumpkin patch?

Yes. The farm is always reservation-only. You can book online and pick a time. Reservations with pizza open every Tuesday at 5:00 PM for the upcoming weekend.

What days can I visit?

Farm-fun-only reservations are available Thursday through Sunday. Reservations with Wise Pie pizza are Friday through Sunday.

What does a pumpkin patch visit cost?

For fall 2026, farm fun without pizza is $3 per person for ages 3 and up, and infants 2 and under are free. Wagon rides are $4.50 per person, the corn pit is $4 per person and the barrel train is $3 per child.

Are dogs allowed at the farm?

Unfortunately, no. Because of liability and food-safety regulations, pets must stay home. Service animals are welcome.
